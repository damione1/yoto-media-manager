# YotoMediaManager

The songs they ask for. The stories in your language. All on their Yoto.

Yoto has a wonderful catalogue, with hundreds of stories, songs and adventures to discover. But my two-year-old had a much shorter wish list: a few favourite nursery rhymes, on repeat. The ones we loved were on YouTube, and I wanted to hear them coming from our Yoto.

Much of the content I found was in English or aimed at older children. Sometimes, what your family wants is a particular song, a familiar story in another language, or simply something that isn't in the catalogue.

Thankfully, Yoto lets families bring their own audio and provides an [API](https://yoto.dev/) for developers to build on. I'm grateful they left that door open. It made this app possible.

I built **YotoMediaManager** to bring the whole process into one place: find the audio, import it from YouTube or a file, trim away the intro or credits, and add it to a Yoto playlist. Less moving between tools. More time listening to the songs your little one actually wants.

That's how this app started: one parent, a toddler, and a handful of favourite songs. I hope it helps your family fill its Yoto with its own favourites, too.

With YotoMediaManager, you can:

- Import audio files or paste a YouTube link.
- Listen and trim your audio while keeping the original.
- Create and edit playlists directly on your Yoto account.
- Choose Yoto's pixel art icons or upload your own images.
- Choose a player and control playback from the app.

For **macOS and Windows**. Follow [Releases](https://github.com/damione1/yoto-media-manager/releases) for downloads.
