# Players and groups

## Choose a player

Connect your account, open `Lecteurs`, and refresh the data with `Actualiser depuis Yoto`. Select the player you want to control. Actions apply to that player.

A player must be connected to receive a command. If its state looks outdated, refresh again and check its Wi-Fi connection.

From a playlist in `Mes playlists Yoto`, choose `Lire sur le lecteur sélectionné`. The player panel offers the available controls, including pause, resume, stop, volume, and a timer.

## Understand confirmations

Sending a command does not always mean playback or a setting change has been confirmed. The result may report that a command was sent, accepted by the player, or observed in its state. Check the displayed result and player state before repeating an action.

Some features, such as lighting or Bluetooth, depend on the model and the API's capabilities. An unavailable feature shows an explanation. Detailed state may require an additional permission.

## Settings and shortcuts

Choose `Charger les réglages du lecteur` before changing settings. Adjust the values you want, then choose `Enregistrer ces réglages`. The app reads the configuration again to verify the result.

Day and night shortcuts are shown separately. Changing one does not erase the other. Saving an empty selection for one mode explicitly removes its shortcuts. Some predefined Yoto shortcuts may be visible but cannot be edited.

## Organize groups

In `Groupes familiaux`, create a group or choose `Modifier`. Give it a name, select the creations to include, and use the arrows to adjust their order. Save the group when your selection is complete.

You can add an image or choose from your family's images. Removing an image may be unavailable depending on Yoto's documented capabilities.

Deleting a group removes that organization. It does not delete your original audio from the local library.
