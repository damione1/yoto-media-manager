# Build a playlist

## Find your Yoto playlists

Connect your account in `Compte`, then open `Playlists`. Your Make Your Own creations load automatically from Yoto at startup and after login. Click a title to open its current content.

The list represents your Yoto playlists. A cache keeps the latest loaded data available; audio files you prepare stay in the local library. `Actualiser depuis Yoto` reloads changes made elsewhere.

## Add audio

1. Open your playlist and choose `Ajouter des médias`.
2. In the dialog, check the audio files you want from your library.
3. Choose each file's version if you have prepared more than one.
4. Choose `Ajouter` to send the selection to Yoto.
5. Wait for confirmation. Progress stays visible during preparation and upload, then the tracks appear in the playlist.

`Importer` opens the `Imports` screen so you can add a file or YouTube link to your library. Prepare that audio, then return to the playlist and select it. Importing into the library does not automatically add media to a playlist.

Versions already uploaded to the active account are reused. A new trim may require another upload. The original remains on your computer.

## Create and edit

Enter a title and choose `Créer une playlist`. The playlist is created on Yoto, and the editor opens after confirmation, even if it has no audio yet.

Confirm a title change with Enter or by leaving the field. Changes to titles, metadata, order, illustrations, and settings are sent directly to Yoto. There is no separate local save or publish step.

A progress indicator shows the current operation. The displayed playlist keeps its confirmed content until the result arrives. If an error occurs, the message and task details explain what still needs attention.

## Choose illustrations

A cover represents the playlist. Small icons represent its chapters or tracks on the player.

Choose `Changer l’icône` from the audio's menu. The dialog offers the `Yoto` catalog and your personal images in `Mes icônes`. Select an icon to apply it, or import an image to add it to your personal icons. The change is sent to Yoto with a progress indicator.

A song displays a single title. Playlists with chapters containing multiple tracks keep that structure; their detailed settings remain available in the menus.

## Choose playback behavior

Linear playback follows the chapters and tracks in order. End-of-track settings let playback continue to the next track, repeat, or stop.

Events and random playback ranges are advanced settings. Some order changes are blocked if they would make an event reference ambiguous. Update the affected events before continuing.

## Duplicate or delete

In `Plus`, duplication creates a new playlist on Yoto. Deleting a playlist removes it from your Yoto account and keeps your original audio in the library.

## Changes elsewhere or an uncertain upload

Opening or refreshing a playlist reads its content from Yoto again. If another app changed it during your operation, a conflict may require a choice. Your edits are preserved to let you resolve it.

If Yoto may have accepted an upload but its response did not arrive, open the task details in `Imports` and choose `Vérifier auprès de Yoto`. The app checks the result before allowing another upload, to avoid duplicates.
